# Botanical Garden

A vibrant organic design system inspired by natural flora — lush, warm, and grounded.

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|------|------|-----|
| Rich natural green | Fern Green | `#4a7c59` |
| Bright floral accent | Marigold | `#f9a620` |
| Earthy warm tone | Terracotta | `#b7472a` |
| Soft neutral backgrounds | Cream | `#f5f3ed` |

## Typography

- **Headers**: DejaVu Serif Bold
- **Body**: DejaVu Sans

## Recommended Applications

Garden retailers, culinary presentations, farm-to-table content, botanical brands, natural products.
