# Ocean Depths

A professional maritime-themed design that evokes the serenity of deep ocean waters.

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|------|------|-----|
| Primary background | Deep Navy | `#1a2332` |
| Accent / highlights | Teal | `#2d8b8b` |
| Secondary accent | Seafoam | `#a8dadc` |
| Text / light backgrounds | Cream | `#f1faee` |

## Typography

- **Headers**: DejaVu Sans Bold
- **Body**: DejaVu Sans

## Recommended Applications

Corporate presentations, financial reports, professional consulting decks, trust-building content.
